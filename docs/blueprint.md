# **App Name**: CrediSoluções

## Core Features:

- Hero Section Impact: Displays a high-impact title and customer-focused message to immediately engage visitors with a clear value proposition.
- WhatsApp Integration: Provides direct WhatsApp contact through prominent buttons, streamlining communication and lead generation.
- Personal Presentation: Showcases a professional image with circular format for an enhanced personal touch and brand identity.
- Animated Portfolio: Presents work samples with engaging fade-in animations from left to right, highlighting key projects dynamically as users scroll.
- Success Showcase: Visually reinforces credibility by displaying customer success stories, focusing on positive outcomes such as car acquisitions and successful loans.
- Service Highlights with Icons: Lists key services like personal loans, vehicle financing, and mortgage refinancing using representative icons to quickly inform and guide users.
- Contact Form with WhatsApp Option: Captures leads efficiently through a streamlined form, which offers users the choice to submit their details via email or initiate a direct WhatsApp conversation for quicker follow-up.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Blue (#007AFF) for backgrounds and main texts to convey trust and professionalism, referencing the request.
- Secondary color: Orange (#FF9500) for buttons and secondary titles to add energy and call to attention, referencing the request.
- Accent color: Gold (#FFD700) specifically for WhatsApp buttons to maximize emphasis, referencing the request.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', a modern serif, to give the website an elegant and fashionable aesthetic, paired with 'PT Sans' a modern yet warm sans-serif for the body.
- Custom icons for services related to credit and financing.
- Mobile-first design with a clean, professional, conversion-focused layout.
- Subtle animations when scrolling through different website sections.
